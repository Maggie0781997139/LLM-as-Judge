GULU UNIVERSITY – ADMISSIONS POLICY AND REQUIREMENTS
=====================================================

1. GENERAL ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

All applicants seeking admission to Gulu University must meet the following minimum requirements:

a) Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) with at least 5 passes obtained at one sitting.
b) Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) with at least 2 principal passes obtained at the same sitting.
c) A valid national identification document or passport.
d) Two recent passport-sized photographs.

2. UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMMES

2.1 Direct Entry
Applicants must have obtained a minimum of 2 principal passes at UACE in subjects relevant to the programme of choice. The following cut-off points apply:

- Bachelor of Science: Minimum of 2 principal passes in science subjects (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics)
- Bachelor of Arts: Minimum of 2 principal passes in arts subjects
- Bachelor of Business Administration: Minimum of 2 principal passes including Economics or Mathematics
- Bachelor of Education: Minimum of 2 principal passes in teaching subjects
- Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery (MBChB): Minimum of 3 principal passes in Biology, Chemistry, and Physics or Mathematics

2.2 Mature Age Entry
Applicants aged 25 years and above who do not meet direct entry requirements may apply through the Mature Age Entry Scheme. Requirements include:
- Proof of age (birth certificate or national ID)
- At least 5 years of relevant work experience
- Passing the university's Mature Age Entry Examination

3. APPLICATION PROCEDURE

3.1 Online Application
All applications must be submitted through the university's online application portal at https://apply.gu.ac.ug. The application fee is UGX 50,000 (fifty thousand Uganda shillings) for Ugandan nationals and USD 50 for international applicants.

3.2 Required Documents
Applicants must upload the following documents:
- Certified copies of academic certificates (UCE, UACE, or equivalent)
- National identification document or passport
- Two passport-sized photographs
- Recommendation letter from a referee

3.3 Application Deadlines
- Government-sponsored students: Applications open in February and close in April each year.
- Private-sponsored students: Applications open in February and close in July each year.
- Second intake (for selected programmes): Applications open in June and close in October.

4. INTERNATIONAL APPLICANTS

International applicants must have qualifications equivalent to the Uganda education system. Equivalence is determined by the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) or the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE).

Additional requirements for international applicants:
- Valid study permit/visa
- Proof of English language proficiency (for applicants from non-English speaking countries)
- Certified translations of academic documents not in English

5. POSTGRADUATE ADMISSIONS

5.1 Master's Programmes
- A good first degree (at least Second Class Upper Division) from a recognised university
- Two academic reference letters
- A research concept note of not more than 5 pages (for research-based programmes)

5.2 PhD Programmes
- A Master's degree from a recognised university
- A detailed research proposal of not more than 15 pages
- Evidence of prior research (publications, conference papers)
- Three academic reference letters

6. ADMISSION DECISIONS

The University Admissions Board meets in August each year to consider applications for the main intake. Applicants are notified of admission decisions through:
- Email notification to the address provided in the application
- Publication on the university website
- SMS notification to the mobile number provided

Successful applicants must confirm their acceptance within 14 days of receiving the admission offer by paying the required fees.

7. APPEALS

Applicants who are not satisfied with an admission decision may appeal in writing to the Academic Registrar within 30 days of the decision. The Appeals Committee meets within 21 days of receiving the appeal.
