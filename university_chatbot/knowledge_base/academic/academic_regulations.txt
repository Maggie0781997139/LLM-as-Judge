GULU UNIVERSITY – ACADEMIC REGULATIONS
========================================

1. ACADEMIC CALENDAR

1.1 The academic year consists of two semesters:
    - Semester I: August to December
    - Semester II: January to May

1.2 Each semester comprises 17 weeks:
    - 15 weeks of instruction
    - 2 weeks of examinations

2. COURSE REGISTRATION

2.1 All students must register for courses online through the student portal at https://portal.gu.ac.ug within the first two weeks of each semester.

2.2 A normal course load is 5 course units per semester, totalling 15-18 credit hours.

2.3 Students wishing to take more than 6 course units (overload) must obtain written approval from the Dean of their faculty.

2.4 Late registration attracts a penalty fee of UGX 50,000 and is only permitted up to the end of the third week.

2.5 Course changes (add/drop) are permitted within the first three weeks of the semester. After this period, a student may only withdraw from a course with a "W" grade.

3. ATTENDANCE POLICY

3.1 Students are required to attend at least 75% of lectures, tutorials, and practical sessions for each course.

3.2 Students who fail to meet the 75% attendance requirement shall be barred from sitting the final examination in that course.

3.3 Absence due to illness must be supported by a medical certificate from the University Health Centre or a recognised medical facility, submitted within 5 working days.

4. ASSESSMENT AND EXAMINATIONS

4.1 Assessment Structure
Each course is assessed as follows:
- Coursework (tests, assignments, practicals): 40% of the final grade
- Final examination: 60% of the final grade

4.2 Coursework
- At least two coursework assessments must be conducted per course per semester.
- Coursework marks must be displayed on the departmental notice board before the examination period.

4.3 Examinations
- University examinations are held at the end of each semester.
- Examinations are conducted under the supervision of the University Examinations Board.
- Students must present their valid university identity card to be admitted to the examination room.
- Mobile phones and electronic devices are strictly prohibited in examination rooms.

4.4 Grading System

| Letter Grade | Marks (%)   | Grade Point | Description              |
|--------------|-------------|-------------|--------------------------|
| A            | 80 – 100    | 5.0         | Excellent                |
| B+           | 75 – 79     | 4.5         | Very Good                |
| B            | 70 – 74     | 4.0         | Good                     |
| C+           | 65 – 69     | 3.5         | Fairly Good              |
| C            | 60 – 64     | 3.0         | Fair                     |
| C-           | 55 – 59     | 2.5         | Marginal Pass            |
| D+           | 50 – 54     | 2.0         | Pass                     |
| D            | 45 – 49     | 1.5         | Marginal Fail            |
| F            | Below 45    | 0.0         | Fail                     |

4.5 Pass Mark
The minimum pass mark for undergraduate courses is 50% (Grade D+).
The minimum pass mark for postgraduate courses is 60% (Grade C).

5. PROGRESSION RULES

5.1 A student must pass at least 75% of the registered course units in a semester to progress to the next semester.

5.2 A student who fails more than 50% of the registered course units in two consecutive semesters shall be discontinued from the university.

5.3 Retake Examinations
- Students who fail a course are permitted to retake the examination at the next available opportunity.
- A maximum of two retake attempts is allowed per course.
- The maximum grade achievable on a retake is B (70%).

6. GRADUATION REQUIREMENTS

6.1 To qualify for graduation, a student must:
    a) Complete all required course units for their programme.
    b) Achieve a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 2.0.
    c) Complete the required research project or dissertation (where applicable).
    d) Have no outstanding financial obligations to the university.
    e) Have spent the minimum required duration for the programme:
       - Bachelor's degree: 3-5 years depending on the programme
       - Master's degree: 2 years minimum
       - PhD: 3 years minimum

6.2 Classification of Degrees

| Classification               | CGPA Range    |
|------------------------------|---------------|
| First Class Honours          | 4.4 – 5.0    |
| Second Class Honours (Upper) | 3.6 – 4.3    |
| Second Class Honours (Lower) | 2.8 – 3.5    |
| Pass                         | 2.0 – 2.7    |

7. ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

7.1 The university maintains a zero-tolerance policy on academic dishonesty, including:
- Plagiarism
- Cheating in examinations
- Fabrication of data
- Collusion in individual assignments

7.2 Penalties for academic dishonesty:
- First offence: Zero mark for the assessment and formal warning
- Second offence: Failure of the entire course
- Third offence: Expulsion from the university

8. TRANSCRIPTS AND CERTIFICATES

8.1 Academic transcripts can be requested from the Academic Registrar's Office.
8.2 Processing time: 5-10 working days from the date of application.
8.3 Fees:
    - Official transcript: UGX 20,000 per copy
    - Certified copy of degree certificate: UGX 50,000
    - Verification letter: UGX 10,000

8.4 Transcript requests can be submitted:
    - In person at the Academic Registrar's Office, Administration Block, Room 201
    - By email to transcripts@gu.ac.ug
    - Through the student portal
