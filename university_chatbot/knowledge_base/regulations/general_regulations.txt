GULU UNIVERSITY – GENERAL REGULATIONS AND CODE OF CONDUCT
============================================================

1. STUDENT REGISTRATION

1.1 All students must register at the beginning of each semester through the student portal.
1.2 Registration is only complete upon payment of the required fees.
1.3 Students who fail to register within the stipulated period shall be deemed to have withdrawn from the university.

2. DRESS CODE

2.1 Students are expected to dress modestly and appropriately at all times while on campus.
2.2 The following are not permitted:
    - Transparent or revealing clothing
    - Clothing with offensive language or imagery
    - Slippers in lecture rooms and laboratories

3. CODE OF CONDUCT

3.1 Students must conduct themselves in a manner that upholds the dignity and reputation of the university.

3.2 The following are considered serious offences:
    a) Assault or threatening behaviour towards any member of the university community
    b) Theft or damage to university property
    c) Possession, use, or distribution of illegal drugs
    d) Possession of weapons on campus
    e) Sexual harassment or gender-based violence
    f) Inciting violence or participating in riots
    g) Forgery of university documents
    h) Unauthorized entry into restricted areas

3.3 Disciplinary Process
    - Reports of misconduct are submitted to the Dean of Students
    - A preliminary investigation is conducted within 7 days
    - The student is given written notice of the charges and an opportunity to respond
    - A Disciplinary Committee hearing is held within 21 days
    - The student may appeal to the Vice Chancellor within 14 days of the decision

3.4 Penalties
    - Written warning
    - Community service (up to 40 hours)
    - Suspension (up to 2 semesters)
    - Expulsion from the university
    - Criminal prosecution (for offences that constitute crimes)

4. USE OF UNIVERSITY FACILITIES

4.1 University facilities are for the exclusive use of registered students, staff, and authorized visitors.

4.2 Smoking is prohibited in all indoor spaces and within 10 metres of building entrances.

4.3 Alcohol consumption is prohibited on campus except at designated events approved by the Vice Chancellor.

4.4 Pets and animals are not permitted on campus, except for service animals.

5. VEHICLE AND PARKING REGULATIONS

5.1 Students with vehicles must register them with the Security Office and obtain a parking permit.
5.2 Parking is available in designated student parking areas only.
5.3 The speed limit on campus is 20 km/h.
5.4 The university is not responsible for loss or damage to vehicles parked on campus.

6. INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY POLICY

6.1 University IT resources are provided for academic and research purposes.
6.2 Prohibited activities include:
    - Accessing or distributing pornographic material
    - Hacking or attempting to access restricted systems
    - Using university networks for commercial purposes
    - Downloading copyrighted material illegally
    - Sending spam or malicious emails
6.3 Violations may result in suspension of IT privileges and disciplinary action.

7. PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION

7.1 The university collects and processes student data in accordance with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.
7.2 Student records are confidential and are only shared with authorized university officials.
7.3 Students have the right to access their personal data and request corrections.
7.4 Data protection inquiries should be directed to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@gu.ac.ug.

8. GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE

8.1 Students with grievances may submit them to:
    - Course level: Course instructor or Head of Department
    - Faculty level: Dean of Faculty
    - University level: Dean of Students or Academic Registrar
    - Final appeal: Vice Chancellor

8.2 All grievances should be submitted in writing and will be acknowledged within 5 working days.
8.3 Resolution is expected within 30 working days of submission.
