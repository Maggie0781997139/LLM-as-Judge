4. PAYMENT SCHEDULE

4.1 All fees must be paid before the beginning of each semester.
4.2 Students who cannot pay in full may apply for an instalment plan:
    - 60% of total fees at registration
    - 40% before mid-semester examinations
4.3 Late payment attracts a penalty of UGX 50,000 per week.

5. PAYMENT METHODS

Fees can be paid through:
- Bank deposit at Stanbic Bank, Account Number: 9030005678901, Account Name: Gulu University Fees Collection
- Mobile Money: Dial *165*3# and follow the prompts (reference: student registration number)
- Online payment via the student portal at https://portal.gu.ac.ug

6. SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL AID

6.1 Government Scholarships
The Government of Uganda sponsors students through the District Quota System. Each district is allocated a number of slots based on population. Selection is based on UACE performance.

6.2 University Scholarships
Gulu University offers merit-based scholarships to the top 5% of students in each faculty:
- Full scholarship: covers 100% of tuition fees
- Partial scholarship: covers 50% of tuition fees
These scholarships are renewable each year provided the student maintains a CGPA of at least 4.0 out of 5.0.

6.3 External Scholarships
The university facilitates applications for external scholarships including:
- MasterCard Foundation Scholars Program
- DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service)
- Commonwealth Scholarships
- African Development Bank Scholarships

Students can inquire about available scholarships at the Financial Aid Office located in the Administration Block, Room 105.

7. REFUND POLICY

7.1 Students who withdraw within the first 2 weeks of the semester are eligible for an 80% refund.
7.2 Withdrawal between weeks 3 and 4 qualifies for a 50% refund.
7.3 No refunds are available after week 4 of the semester.
7.4 Refund requests must be submitted in writing to the University Bursar.
