GULU UNIVERSITY – STUDENT SERVICES GUIDE
==========================================

1. STUDENT ACCOMMODATION

1.1 On-Campus Accommodation
Gulu University provides on-campus accommodation in the following halls of residence:

- Africa Hall (Male): Capacity 400 students
- Nile Hall (Female): Capacity 350 students
- Peace Hall (Mixed – Postgraduate): Capacity 150 students

Room types and rates per semester:
- Single room: UGX 800,000
- Double room (shared): UGX 500,000 per student
- Triple room (shared): UGX 350,000 per student

1.2 Application for Accommodation
- First-year students are given priority for on-campus accommodation.
- Applications are submitted through the student portal during the admission acceptance process.
- Allocation is on a first-come, first-served basis.
- Students must pay the full accommodation fee before checking in.

1.3 Off-Campus Housing
The Dean of Students' Office maintains a list of approved off-campus housing options within a 5 km radius of the university. Approved hostels are inspected annually for safety and hygiene standards. The list is available at the Dean of Students' Office, Student Centre, Room 102.

2. LIBRARY SERVICES

2.1 Main Library
The university's main library is located in the Library Complex, opposite the Administration Block.

Operating hours:
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Sunday: 2:00 PM – 8:00 PM
- During examination periods: 7:00 AM – 12:00 AM (midnight)

2.2 Library Resources
- Over 50,000 print volumes
- Access to electronic databases: JSTOR, PubMed, Google Scholar, African Journals Online
- 120 computer workstations with internet access
- Printing and photocopying services (UGX 200 per page)
- Group study rooms (bookable through the library website)
- Quiet study areas on the 2nd and 3rd floors

2.3 Borrowing Policy
- Undergraduate students: Up to 4 books for 14 days
- Postgraduate students: Up to 6 books for 21 days
- Academic staff: Up to 10 books for 30 days
- Late return fee: UGX 500 per book per day
- Lost book: Replacement cost plus UGX 20,000 processing fee

3. HEALTH SERVICES

3.1 University Health Centre
Location: Adjacent to Africa Hall
Operating hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Emergency services: Available 24/7

3.2 Services Provided
- General outpatient consultations
- First aid and emergency care
- Laboratory tests (basic)
- Pharmacy (subsidised medication)
- Health education and counselling
- HIV/AIDS voluntary counselling and testing
- Immunisation services

3.3 Referrals
Students requiring specialised care are referred to Gulu Regional Referral Hospital (2 km from campus) or Lacor Hospital (5 km from campus).

3.4 Health Insurance
All students are required to have valid health insurance. The university offers a group health insurance scheme at UGX 100,000 per academic year, covering outpatient and inpatient care up to UGX 5,000,000.

4. COUNSELLING AND GUIDANCE

4.1 The Counselling Centre is located in the Student Centre, Room 110.
4.2 Services include:
- Academic counselling
- Career guidance and planning
- Personal and emotional counselling
- Peer mentoring programmes
- Stress management workshops
4.3 All counselling sessions are confidential.
4.4 Students can book appointments through the student portal or by visiting the centre.

5. SPORTS AND RECREATION

5.1 Facilities
- Football pitch (FIFA-standard artificial turf)
- Basketball and netball courts
- Volleyball courts
- Athletics track (400m)
- Indoor gymnasium
- Swimming pool (Olympic-size, 50m)

5.2 Sports Clubs
The university supports the following sports clubs:
- Football, Basketball, Netball, Volleyball, Athletics, Swimming, Chess, Table Tennis, Badminton

5.3 Inter-University Competitions
Gulu University participates in the East African University Games and the Uganda University Sports Association (UUSA) championships.

6. STUDENT ORGANISATIONS

6.1 Students' Guild
The Students' Guild is the official student government. Elections are held annually in March. All registered students are members of the guild.

6.2 Clubs and Societies
Over 30 registered student clubs and societies, including:
- Debate Society
- Drama and Cultural Society
- Entrepreneurship Club
- Environmental Conservation Club
- Red Cross Society
- Religious organisations (Christian Union, Islamic Society)
- Technology and Innovation Club

6.3 To register a new club, submit an application to the Dean of Students with at least 20 founding members and a faculty advisor.

7. CAREER SERVICES

7.1 The Career Services Centre is located in the Administration Block, Room 305.
7.2 Services include:
- Career counselling and aptitude testing
- CV/resume writing workshops
- Mock interview sessions
- Job placement assistance
- Internship coordination
- Employer networking events (held bi-annually in March and October)

7.3 The university maintains partnerships with over 100 employers across Uganda for internship and job placements.

8. ICT SERVICES

8.1 Wi-Fi is available across all campus buildings. Students connect using their university email credentials.
8.2 Computer labs are located in:
- Library Complex (120 workstations)
- Faculty of Science (60 workstations)
- Faculty of Business (40 workstations)
8.3 Student email: All students receive a university email address (format: firstname.lastname@student.gu.ac.ug).
8.4 The ICT Help Desk is located in the Library Complex, Ground Floor. Operating hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Email: ict.help@gu.ac.ug.
